Why a pet weather app?

Because the forecast decides your pet's whole day. It sets when you walk, how long you stay out, whether the pavement is safe for paws, and whether tonight is a thundershirt night. WeatherPets puts your own dog or cat in front of that forecast — real Apple WeatherKit data, severe-weather alerts, and home screen widgets starring the pet you already check on more often than the sky.

It's also why we write. The WeatherPets blog is a growing library of weather-smart pet care, built on primary veterinary sources. Start with the guides owners search for most: what temperature is safe for a dog walk, the warning signs of dog heatstroke, the 7-second pavement test for summer paws, and how cold is too cold for dogs.

Then go deeper by topic: pet safety & weather, breed weather guides, seasonal care, weather gear reviews, and app guides & comparisons — or browse the full blog. Curious who's behind it? Meet Dom and Adam.

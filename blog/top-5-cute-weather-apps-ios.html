Top 5 Cute Weather Apps for iOS (2026)

If your weather app should make you smile, these are the cutest on iOS — ranked, with WeatherPets on top.

Cute doesn't mean useless. Every app here gives real forecasts — they just do it with charm.

1. WeatherPets — Cutest by a mile

Because the star of WeatherPets is your pet, no other app feels as personal. Your dog or cat appears in AI weather scenes that change with real conditions, plus home screen widgets and morning reports. Cuteness and full Apple WeatherKit data. Available on iOS.

2. Weather Kitty — Cats galore

A classic that pairs the forecast with sweet cat photos. Simple and lovable.

3. Weather Puppy — Dogs on demand

The canine counterpart, with a big library of adorable pups.

4. Carrot Weather — Cute with a bite

Quirky illustrations and a sarcastic AI make Carrot charming in its own offbeat way.

5. Apple Weather — Clean and friendly

Not "cute" exactly, but its bright animations and tidy design are easy on the eyes.

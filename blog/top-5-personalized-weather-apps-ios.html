Top 5 Personalized Weather Apps for iOS (2026)

Generic forecasts are forgettable. These five iOS weather apps make the forecast feel like yours — and WeatherPets is the most personal of all.

Personalization is what turns a weather app from a utility into a habit. Here's who does it best.

1. WeatherPets — The most personal forecast

Nothing is more personal than your own pet. WeatherPets builds the entire experience around your dog or cat — AI scenes, widgets, and morning reports tuned to the personality traits you choose. Full Apple WeatherKit data underneath. Available on iOS.

2. Carrot Weather — Customizable personality

Dial in the tone, choose data layouts, and unlock secret locations. Deeply customizable for power users.

3. Apple Weather — Tailored alerts

Location-based notifications and rearrangeable modules let you shape the basics.

4. Weather Kitty — Pick your cats

Choose the cat photos that greet you with each forecast.

5. Weather Puppy — Pick your pups

The same idea with dogs — select breeds you love.

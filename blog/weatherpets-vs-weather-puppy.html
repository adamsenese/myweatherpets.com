WeatherPets vs Weather Puppy

Both put a happy dog in your forecast. The real difference: WeatherPets uses your dog.

Weather Puppy helped invent the cute-dog weather genre. WeatherPets personalizes it. Here's the breakdown.

The pet on screen

Weather Puppy rotates through adorable stock dog photos. WeatherPets features your actual pup — upload a photo and AI builds weather-accurate scenes around your dog.

Weather data

Both cover the basics. WeatherPets is powered by Apple WeatherKit for real, accurate forecasts and severe-weather alerts.

Widgets & reports

WeatherPets puts your dog and the current temperature on your home screen, and delivers morning reports tuned to your dog's personality. Style packs let you reskin scenes in pixel art, watercolor, and more.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose Weather Puppy for a simple app with a big library of cute dogs.
  • Choose WeatherPets for your own dog, plus widgets, alerts, and personalization.

If you'd rather see your own best friend every morning, WeatherPets is the pick — and it handles cats too. Try it today.

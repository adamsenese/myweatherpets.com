Top 5 iPhone Widgets for Pet Owners (2026)

A great widget puts what matters on your home screen. For pet owners, these five are the best on iPhone in 2026.

Widgets turn your home screen into a glanceable dashboard. Here are the five pet owners should add first.

1. WeatherPets widget — Your pet + the temperature

The WeatherPets widget shows your real dog or cat alongside the current temperature and conditions, updating throughout the day. It's the only weather widget that's also a photo of your best friend — useful and heart-warming at a glance. Available on iOS.

2. Apple Weather widget — Reliable basics

Clean current-conditions and forecast widgets in several sizes. A dependable default.

3. Chewy widget — Reorder reminders

Keep track of upcoming deliveries so you never run out of food.

4. AllTrails widget — Trail at a glance

Quick access to saved dog-friendly trails for spontaneous walks.

5. 11pets widget — Care reminders

See upcoming vet visits and medication times without opening the app.

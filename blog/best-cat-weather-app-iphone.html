The Best Cat Weather App for iPhone

Cat people deserve a weather app as charming as their cat. WeatherPets makes your feline the star of the forecast.

Cats may not need walks, but cat owners still check the weather constantly. WeatherPets makes that habit a daily delight by putting your cat front and center.

Why WeatherPets is the best cat weather app

  • Your cat in every scene. Upload a photo and AI re-imagines your cat in weather-accurate settings.
  • Real forecasts. Accurate forecasts and severe-weather alerts powered by Apple WeatherKit.
  • Home screen widget. Your cat plus the current temperature at a glance.
  • Morning reports. A daily briefing written in your cat's (let's be honest, slightly judgmental) personality.
  • Style packs. Pixel art, watercolor, studio photography, and more.

Cute and capable

Behind the charm is a complete weather app. You won't trade accuracy for adorableness — you get both. And if you have a dog too, WeatherPets covers them in the same app.

The Best Weather App for Pet Owners

If you love your pet and check the weather every day, the best weather app for you is the one that brings the two together. That's WeatherPets.

Pet owners aren't ordinary weather-app users. We plan walks around the rain, worry about hot pavement in summer, and bundle up small dogs in winter. A weather app built with pets in mind just fits our day better.

What makes a weather app great for pet owners

  • Accurate, walk-ready forecasts. The everyday details that decide whether it's a good time to head outside.
  • Severe-weather alerts. Timely warnings so you and your pet stay safe.
  • A reason to actually open it. Personalization keeps you engaged with the forecast instead of ignoring it.

Why WeatherPets leads

WeatherPets delivers real, accurate forecasts powered by Apple WeatherKit — then wraps them around your real pet. Upload a photo, add a few personality traits, and your dog or cat becomes your forecaster. Home screen widgets show your pet and the current temperature at a glance, and morning reports give you the day ahead in your pet's voice.

It's the rare app that's genuinely useful and a daily delight. The weather data stands on its own; the pet makes you want to check it.

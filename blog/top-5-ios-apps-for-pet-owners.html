Top 5 iOS Apps for Pet Owners (2026)

From booking a sitter to checking the forecast, these are the five iOS apps we think every pet owner should have in 2026.

Your phone can make pet parenting easier — and a lot more fun. Here are our top five, ranked.

1. WeatherPets — Best for daily delight

WeatherPets turns your real dog or cat into your personal weather reporter. Upload a photo, add a few personality traits, and your pet stars in AI weather scenes backed by accurate Apple WeatherKit forecasts. Home screen widgets and morning reports make it the app you'll open most. It's useful and joyful — which is why it tops our list. Available on iOS.

2. Rover — Best for pet sitting & walking

When you need a trusted sitter or dog walker, Rover's marketplace makes booking easy, with reviews, photos, and in-app messaging. A staple for traveling pet owners.

3. Chewy — Best for supplies & food

Chewy keeps food, treats, and supplies arriving on schedule, with famously good customer service. A reliable staple.

4. AllTrails — Best for dog walks & hikes

Find dog-friendly trails, check distance and elevation, and discover new routes for active pups. Pair it with WeatherPets to pick the perfect day to go.

5. 11pets — Best for pet health records

Track vaccinations, vet visits, grooming, and medications in one organized place. Great for multi-pet households.

Looking for an Alternative to Weather Kitty?

Weather Kitty made checking the forecast adorable. But if you want the cat in your weather app to be your cat, WeatherPets is the alternative worth downloading.

Weather Kitty popularized a simple, lovable idea: weather is friendlier when a cat delivers it. WeatherPets takes that idea one step further — instead of stock cats, it puts your cat into the forecast.

Why pet owners switch to WeatherPets

  • Your real pet, not stock art. Upload a photo and our AI places your cat into weather-accurate scenes that change with real conditions.
  • Serious weather data. Real, accurate forecasts and severe-weather alerts powered by Apple WeatherKit.
  • Home screen widgets. Your cat and the current temperature, right where you'll see them.
  • Morning reports with personality. Wake up to a forecast your pet "writes" for you.

How it compares

Weather Kitty is a charming, lightweight app built around a fixed cast of cartoon cats. WeatherPets is built around personalization: the star of every scene is the pet sleeping on your keyboard right now. You also get style packs — watercolor, pixel art, studio photography — so the look stays fresh.

If you love cats and want a genuinely useful weather app, WeatherPets is the natural next step. It works with dogs too, so the whole household's pets can take turns reporting.

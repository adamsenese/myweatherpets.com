The Best Pet Apps for iPhone in 2026

From adoption to everyday care, these are the best pet apps for iPhone in 2026 — ranked, starting with the one we'd put on every pet owner's home screen.

The App Store is full of pet apps. These five earn their spot on your home screen.

1. WeatherPets — The everyday favorite

WeatherPets turns your real pet into your weather reporter, blending full forecasts with AI scenes, widgets, and daily reports. It's the pet app you'll open every single morning. Available on iOS.

2. Rover — Sitters & walkers

Book trusted, reviewed pet sitters and dog walkers in minutes. Essential when life gets busy.

3. Chewy — Food & supplies

Food, treats, and supplies delivered on a schedule, with standout support. Your pantry restocks itself.

4. Petfinder — Adoption

Searching for a new family member? Petfinder lists adoptable pets from shelters and rescues nationwide.

5. 11pets — Health tracking

Keep vaccinations, vet visits, and medication schedules organized — ideal for multi-pet homes.

What Is WeatherPets? The Weather App Starring Your Own Pet

Most weather apps are a wall of numbers you check out of obligation. WeatherPets is the one you actually look forward to, because the forecast comes from a familiar face: your own pet. Here is what the app is, how it works, and why pet owners are making it their daily weather habit.

The one-line version

WeatherPets is an iPhone weather app that turns your real pet into your personal weather reporter. You pick a pet, give it a personality, and your accurate local forecast gets delivered in its voice, sunny days, storms, snow, and all. Same reliable weather you would get anywhere, wrapped in something that makes you smile.

How it works

Setup takes a minute:

  • Pick your pet. Upload a photo of your own dog, cat, guinea pig, or bunny. The AI captures their likeness — coloring, markings, expression — so the app stars the animal you actually live with, not a generic mascot.
  • Give it a personality. Choose traits, from sweet and gentle to dramatic and a little extra, so the forecast sounds like them.
  • Get your forecast. Your pet appears on the home screen in AI-generated scenes restyled for the current conditions, and tells you the day's weather in character. The weather itself comes from Apple WeatherKit, with hourly detail and a 10-day outlook.

If you want the click-by-click version, we wrote a full walkthrough on how to turn your pet into a weather reporter, including tips for picking a photo the AI will love.

What you get

  • A home screen that stars your pet, reimagined for every condition: sun, rain, snow, storms, and night.
  • A full 10-day forecast with hourly detail and live weather tracking, so the cuteness never costs you accuracy.
  • Severe-weather alerts when something serious is headed your way.
  • Home and lock screen widgets in small, medium, and large, with your pet plus the weather at a glance, no need to open the app.
  • Live Activities that track conditions in real time on your lock screen and Dynamic Island.
  • Morning and evening reports, a short daily briefing from your pet to bookend your day.
  • Style packs to change the art direction: Realistic, Cinematic, Lofi Room, Polaroid Adventure, pixel art, watercolor, and studio looks, plus full light and dark mode.
  • A gallery and profile for your pet, restyled across all the weather conditions, with slideshow backgrounds built from your pet's snapshots.

If you want a closer look at the most useful pieces, our WeatherPets widget guide walks through getting your pet onto your home screen, and our deep dive on morning and evening reports covers the daily briefings.

What's free and what's premium

You can try the whole idea for free. The free tier includes one pet profile and a limited number of AI scenes — enough to set up your reporter, see your own pet in the forecast, and decide whether it clicks for you.

Premium runs $9.99 a month or $34.99 a year, and the annual plan is the obvious pick if you stay. It unlocks more scene generation and multiple pet profiles, so a two-dog household can run a proper co-anchor desk. Everything weather-related — the WeatherKit forecasts, the accuracy, the alerts — is real weather data on either tier; premium is about how much of your pet you get, not how much weather.

What about privacy?

Handing an app photos of your pet is a small ask, but it deserves a straight answer. Your photos are stored encrypted in Firebase, they are never used to train AI models, and you can delete them whenever you like. The full details, including everything else the app does and does not collect, are laid out in our privacy policy. Short version: the photos exist to make your scenes, and that is it.

Who it is for

WeatherPets is for pet owners, and honestly for anyone who finds normal weather apps boring. If you love your dog or cat and you check the weather every day anyway, this stitches those two things together. It is also a genuinely useful tool: knowing the day's high, the rain timing, and getting a real-time heads-up helps you plan walks around the heat, prep for a storm, and keep your pet comfortable. The joy is the hook, the planning is the payoff.

Is the weather data accurate?

Yes. WeatherPets is a real forecast app underneath the personality. The data comes from Apple WeatherKit — the same source behind the built-in Weather app — with current conditions, hourly forecasts, a 10-day outlook, live tracking, and severe-weather alerts. The pet is how the information reaches you, not a substitute for it, so you are not trading reliability for cuteness. You get the forecast you actually need, delivered by a face you love.

How it compares

Plenty of weather apps are accurate, and a few are even cute, but WeatherPets is the one built around your real pet rather than a generic mascot or a chart. If you are weighing your options, we put it head to head with the defaults and the heavyweights in our looks at WeatherPets vs the Apple Weather app and the rest of the field in the best pet apps for iPhone in 2026.

How to get it

WeatherPets is available now on the iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later. Download it, pick your pet, and your next forecast will come from the goodest face you know. You can grab it on the App Store — and if you want to see the app in action first, meet our demo forecasters on the WeatherPets News Team page or browse the FAQ.

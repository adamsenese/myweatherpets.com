Looking for an Alternative to Weather Puppy?

Weather Puppy made the forecast wag-worthy. WeatherPets is the alternative that stars your dog instead of stock pups.

Weather Puppy charmed millions by pairing the forecast with a cute dog photo. WeatherPets keeps the joy and adds the one thing fans always wanted: the dog on screen is your dog.

What you get with WeatherPets

  • Your real dog in every scene. Upload a photo and watch our AI place your pup into weather-accurate settings.
  • Full forecasts. Real, accurate forecasts and severe-weather alerts via Apple WeatherKit.
  • Widgets you'll actually use. Your dog plus the temperature on your home screen.
  • Personality-driven reports. Tell us your dog is dramatic and loves treats — the daily briefing follows.

How it compares

Weather Puppy rotates through a library of breed photos. WeatherPets is personalized end to end — and it supports cats too, so no pet gets left out. Style packs let you reskin your dog's scenes in pixel art, watercolor, and more.

If Weather Puppy first made you smile at a forecast, WeatherPets is the upgrade that makes that forecast personal.

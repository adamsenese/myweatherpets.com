The Cutest Weather App on iOS

Plenty of apps claim to be cute. WeatherPets has an unfair advantage: the star of the show is your own pet.

Cuteness is subjective — except when the app stars the dog or cat you already adore. That's why we think WeatherPets is the cutest weather app on iOS, and it's not particularly close.

What makes it the cutest

  • Your real pet, not stock art. Every scene features your actual dog or cat, generated by AI to match real conditions.
  • Scenes that change all day. Morning sun, afternoon rain, evening snow — your pet stars in each one.
  • A widget you'll adore. Your pet plus the temperature, right on your home screen.
  • Personality-packed reports. Daily briefings in your pet's voice.
  • Style packs. Pixel art, watercolor, and studio looks to keep things fresh.

Cute, but also a real weather app

Don't let the charm fool you — WeatherPets runs on Apple WeatherKit with real, accurate forecasts and severe-weather alerts. You get a genuinely good weather app that happens to be the most adorable one on your phone.

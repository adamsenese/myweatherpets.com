WeatherPets vs Weather Kitty

Both apps make the forecast cuter with cats. The difference comes down to one question: do you want stock cats, or your own?

Weather Kitty is a beloved classic. WeatherPets is the personalized next generation. Here's how they compare.

The pet on screen

Weather Kitty pairs forecasts with a library of cute cat photos. WeatherPets stars your cat — upload a photo and AI places your real pet into weather-accurate scenes.

Weather data

Both deliver the essentials. WeatherPets is powered by Apple WeatherKit for real, accurate forecasts and severe-weather alerts.

Widgets & reports

WeatherPets adds home screen widgets showing your cat plus the temperature, and morning reports written in your pet's personality. Style packs (pixel art, watercolor, studio) keep the look fresh.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose Weather Kitty if you want a simple, lightweight app with generic cat charm.
  • Choose WeatherPets if you want your own cat in the forecast, plus widgets, alerts, and customization.

For most cat owners, the personal touch wins. WeatherPets supports dogs too, so the whole household is covered. Try WeatherPets.

Looking for a Weather Kitty alternative?

Weather Kitty popularized a simple, lovable idea: weather is friendlier when a cat delivers it. If you're searching for an alternative, the question is what you want more of. For deeper customization, Carrot Weather leans on personality; for simplicity, Apple Weather is already on your phone. But if what you loved about Weather Kitty was the cat, WeatherPets takes that idea one step further — instead of stock cats, it puts your cat into the forecast, with weather-accurate AI scenes, home screen widgets, and morning reports written around your cat's personality.

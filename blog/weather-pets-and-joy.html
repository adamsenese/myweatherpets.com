Weather Pets & Joy

Checking the weather is a chore. Checking it with your pet on screen is a small daily dose of joy. That's the whole idea behind WeatherPets.

We check the forecast dozens of times a week. It's pure utility — numbers, icons, maybe a radar map. WeatherPets asks a simple question: what if that tiny, repeated moment made you smile instead?

The science of small joys

Psychologists call them "micro-moments" — brief, positive experiences that, repeated daily, lift your baseline mood. Seeing your dog or cat react to the weather is exactly that kind of moment. It's familiar, it's yours, and it shows up right when you reach for your phone in the morning.

How WeatherPets builds joy into the forecast

  • Your real pet, every day. AI scenes feature your actual pet, dressed for the weather outside.
  • Morning reports with personality. A cheerful (or dramatic) briefing from your pet sets the tone.
  • Widgets that greet you. Your pet on the home screen, before you even open the app.
  • Style packs to keep it fresh. New looks mean new little surprises.

None of this comes at the expense of accuracy — you still get real, reliable forecasts and severe-weather alerts powered by Apple WeatherKit. Joy and utility, in the same tap.

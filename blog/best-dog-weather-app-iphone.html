The Best Dog Weather App for iPhone

If you have a dog and an iPhone, the best weather app for you is the one that makes your pup the forecaster. That's WeatherPets.

Dog owners live by the weather — walk timing, hot pavement, rain delays. A dog-first weather app fits the way you already think about the day.

Why WeatherPets is the best dog weather app

  • Your dog in every scene. Upload a photo and AI places your pup into weather-accurate settings that change all day.
  • Walk-ready data. Real, accurate forecasts to time every outing, powered by Apple WeatherKit.
  • Severe-weather alerts. Stay ahead of storms and heat for safer outings.
  • Home screen widget. Your dog plus the temperature, always visible.
  • Morning reports. A daily briefing in your dog's personality.

More than a gimmick

The cuteness is the hook, but the forecast is the real thing. You get the same quality data as any top weather app, wrapped around the dog you love. Style packs — pixel art, watercolor, studio — keep it feeling new.

Top 5 AI Apps for Pet Lovers (2026)

AI is making pet apps smarter and more fun. These five use it best — and WeatherPets leads the pack.

From identifying breeds to reimagining your pet in art, AI is everywhere in pet apps now. Here are our favorites.

1. WeatherPets — Best AI pet scenes

WeatherPets uses AI to place your real pet into weather-accurate scenes that update with live conditions — then layers on style packs like pixel art and watercolor. It's the most delightful, most-used AI pet app we know. Available on iOS.

2. Petfinder — AI-assisted adoption matching

Smart search helps connect you with adoptable pets that fit your home and lifestyle.

3. Dog Scanner — AI breed identification

Point your camera at a dog and let AI estimate its breed mix. Fun and surprisingly accurate.

4. Cat Scanner — AI for cats

The feline equivalent — identify breeds and learn about your cat's traits from a photo.

5. 11pets — Smart health reminders

AI-assisted reminders keep vaccinations and medications on schedule across all your pets.

How to Turn Your Pet Into a Weather Reporter

Turning your pet into your personal forecaster takes just a couple of minutes. Here's the gist.

WeatherPets uses AI to make your real dog or cat the star of your daily forecast. The idea is simple: a little setup up front, then a daily smile every time you check the weather.

It starts with a photo

Add WeatherPets from the App Store, then upload a favorite photo of your pet. The AI captures their likeness so they look like themselves in every scene.

Give them a personality

Tell WeatherPets a little about your pet — playful, dramatic, a treat fanatic. Those traits shape the voice of their morning weather reports, so the forecast feels like it's coming from them.

Let the forecast roll in

With your location set, your pet reports real, accurate weather in AI scenes that change with conditions all day. Add the home screen widget and they greet you with the current temperature before you even open the app.

That's the magic

From there, your pet is your forecaster — rain or shine. Try a pixel art style pack to switch up the look, and see why it's the best weather app for pet owners.

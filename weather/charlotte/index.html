Charlotte weather facts every pet owner should know

  • July average highs in Charlotte hit 90.3F, right at the dangerous walking threshold, and Carolina humidity makes it feel worse, so summer dog walks belong at dawn or after dark.
  • Charlotte winters are kind to dogs: January average highs of 52.3F sit comfortably above the 45F coat line, so midday winter walks are usually the best outing of the day.
  • Snow is a novelty here, averaging just 3.5 inches a year, which means most Charlotte dogs never need booties but also never build tolerance, so keep first snow romps short.
  • Charlotte has hit 104F six times, most recently in the summer 2012 heat wave, a reminder that even shady neighborhood streets can turn paw-burning hot in a Piedmont summer.

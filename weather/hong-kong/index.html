Hong Kong weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Hong Kong summers are long and sticky: June through August average highs of 87.3F to 88.9F with lows above 79F, and sea humidity pushes conditions past the 90F danger line on many afternoons.
  • The Hong Kong Observatory's record low is exactly 32.0F, set on January 18, 1893, so icy paws are a historical footnote here while heat is the everyday concern.
  • Hong Kong's famously steep streets and hill trails add exertion heat: even October, with an average high of 82.6F, calls for water breaks on uphill dog hikes.
  • Winter is the walking jackpot: January averages a 65.7F high and a 58.3F low, comfortable for nearly any breed at any hour of the day.

Hong Kong pet weather questions

May through September. July averages an 88.9F high, well past the 82F risky band, the record is 97.9F, and humidity off the South China Sea makes every degree feel worse. Walk at dawn, choose shaded harbourfront routes over exposed hill climbs, and carry water.
Typhoon season peaks July through September, exactly when average lows still sit near 80.4F. Between storms the air is at its most humid, so keep walks short, and during typhoon signals keep dogs indoors with enrichment games rather than risking flying debris outside.

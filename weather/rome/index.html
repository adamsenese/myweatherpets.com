Rome weather facts every pet owner should know

  • August in Rome averages an 88.9F high, brushing the 90F danger line, so Roman dogs do their real walking at dawn and after sunset in high summer.
  • July brings 337.6 hours of sunshine and only about 2.2 rainy days, which turns cobblestones and pavement into paw-scorchers: use the seven-second back-of-hand test.
  • November is Rome's wettest month at 108.2 mm, more than five times July's 19.8 mm, so autumn walkers need rain gear more than summer walkers ever will.
  • Winter stays walk-friendly: January averages a 53.6F high and a 38.1F low, with coats only needed for small or senior dogs below 45F.

Rome pet weather questions

July and August average highs of 87.8F and 88.9F, well past the 82F risky mark and close to the 90F danger line, so midday summer walks are out. Rome has recorded 105.1F, and stone streets hold heat into the evening, so wait for pavement to cool before the night walk.
January lows average 38.1F, below the 45F threshold where small, senior, and short-coated dogs want a coat, while daytime highs near 53.6F are ideal for long sightseeing walks. The record low of 12.2F is rare cold for Rome.
Autumn is the wet season: November averages 108.2 mm and December 98.3 mm, versus just 19.8 mm in July. Plan for towel-downs and shorter loops from October to December, and enjoy the roughly 74 rainy days a year being clustered outside summer.

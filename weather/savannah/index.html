Savannah weather facts every pet owner should know

  • July in Savannah averages a 92.3F high and a 73.7F low, so even after dark the pavement and air stay warm: dawn is the safest walk slot all summer.
  • Average summer dewpoints run 67.8F to 71.6F, tropical-grade humidity that makes panting less effective, especially for flat-faced breeds like pugs and bulldogs.
  • Snow is so rare that decades can pass between events, and the city averages only about 21 nights below freezing a year, so winter walking gear stays light.
  • January highs average 61.4F, which makes Savannah's famous squares and oak-shaded streets ideal winter territory for long dog walks.

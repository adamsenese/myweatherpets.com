Spokane weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Spokane averages 45.4 inches of snow a year, with December the deepest at 13.8 inches, so Inland Northwest dogs live in booties and paw balm through the holidays.
  • January highs in Spokane average only 34.5F with lows of 24.7F, meaning many midwinter walks happen entirely below freezing: coat up small and senior dogs.
  • Unlike coastal Washington, Spokane summers get genuinely hot: July highs average 84.4F and the record is 109F, set during a June heat event, so hot-day walk timing matters here.
  • Spokane's records stretch from 109F to -30F, a 139 degree spread that makes it one of the more extreme four-season pet cities on the West Coast side of the country.

Edinburgh weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Edinburgh's July high averages just 66.7F, among the coolest of any major European city, so summer overheating is a rare worry for local dogs (record: 88.5F).
  • January lows average 35.1F, above the caution band but below the 45F coat line, so small and senior dogs wear jackets through most of the Scottish winter.
  • Rain arrives on about 128.3 days a year with October the wettest month at 75.7 mm, so waterproof gear beats sunscreen in this city.
  • December offers only 49.2 hours of sunshine all month versus 194.7 in May, so winter walks often happen in twilight: reflective collars and leads are smart kit.

Edinburgh pet weather questions

January lows average 35.1F, so a coat below 45F for small, senior, and short-haired dogs handles a normal winter. Watch for cold snaps though: Edinburgh's record low is 4.1F, well inside dangerous territory, and wind chill off the Forth makes it feel colder.

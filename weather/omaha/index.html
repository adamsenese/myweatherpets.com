Omaha weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Omaha's records stretch from 114F (July 25, 1936) to -32F (January 5, 1884), a 146-degree spread that demands a four-season pet plan.
  • January lows average 15.2F, below the 20F dangerous line, so winter walks mean coats, booties, and 10 to 15 minute outings for most dogs.
  • The city averages 27.1 inches of snow a year, with February the snowiest at 7.8 inches, so keep a towel by the door for snowy paws well into March.
  • Summer is genuinely hot on the Plains: July highs average 88.1F with humid air, so the safest warm-season walks start before the 68.0F average morning low burns off.

Omaha pet weather questions

July and August afternoons, with average highs of 88.1F and 85.8F, sit in the risky-to-dangerous zone, and Missouri River valley humidity makes it feel hotter. Walk early: July mornings average 68.0F.
January lows average 15.2F, below the 20F dangerous threshold, so limit most dogs to short potty breaks on the coldest mornings. Between 20F and 32F, healthy medium and large dogs can walk with caution, while small and senior dogs need coats below 45F.

Louisville weather facts every pet owner should know

  • July highs in Louisville average 89.0F with Ohio Valley humidity on top, so midsummer dog walks are safest at dawn when lows average 70.8F.
  • Louisville's extremes run from 107F down to minus 22F (January 19, 1994), so pets here genuinely need both a summer heat plan and a winter coat.
  • Winter is walkable with gear: January highs average 43.6F, just under the 45F coat threshold for small and senior dogs, and annual snow is a manageable 13.4 inches.
  • The Ohio Valley traps humid air in summer, which makes panting less efficient for dogs; on muggy days treat the site's 82F risky threshold as arriving several degrees early.

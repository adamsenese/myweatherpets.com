Little Rock weather facts every pet owner should know

  • July and August highs in Little Rock average 91.7 and 91.5F, past the 90F danger threshold, so peak summer means dawn-only walks and strict pavement checks.
  • Even Little Rock nights stay hot in midsummer: July lows average 71.2F, so dogs get little overnight relief and water breaks matter on every warm-season outing.
  • Snow barely registers here, just 3.8 inches in an average year with February's 1.6 inches the peak, so ice is an occasional novelty rather than a daily winter hazard.
  • Little Rock's record high of 114F is one of the hottest marks among major state capitals, a reminder that Arkansas heat waves are a serious pet emergency, not just discomfort.

Fresno weather facts every pet owner should know

  • July highs in Fresno average 97.7F, deep in the 90F+ danger zone, so summer dog walks belong at dawn when lows average 69.3F.
  • The heat season is long: average highs stay above 90F from June (91.4F) through September (90.7F), four straight months of hot-pavement paw risk.
  • Fresno's record high of 115F was set on July 8, 1905, and its record low of 17F on January 6, 1913: it has never been truly frigid, but it gets seriously hot.
  • Winters are prime walking season with January highs of 55.4F, no snow to speak of, and cool 40.6F mornings that suit even thick-coated breeds.

Orlando weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Orlando afternoons average 90F or hotter from June through August, peaking at 92.0F in July, so summer dog walks belong at sunrise.
  • Even winter is mild: January highs average 71.8F with lows of 49.5F, prime dog-walking weather that draws snowbirds and their pets.
  • Orlando's official climate normals record no measurable snow, so the paw hazards here are hot pavement and afternoon storms, never ice and salt.
  • Central Florida's summer wet season brings near-daily afternoon thunderstorms, a built-in reason to finish the long walk in the morning and plan indoor play after lunch.

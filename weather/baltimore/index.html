Baltimore weather facts every pet owner should know

  • July highs in Baltimore average 88.8F, and Chesapeake humidity makes it feel stickier, so summer dog walks are best at dawn when lows average 67.7F.
  • Baltimore averages 19.3 inches of snow a year, with February the snowiest month at about 7.5 inches, so keep booties handy for salted Inner Harbor sidewalks.
  • January lows average 25.4F, inside the caution band for dogs, while afternoon highs near 43.2F give a comfortable daily walk window even in midwinter.
  • The city's extremes span 107F (July 10, 1936) to minus 7F, so Baltimore pets see everything from paw-burning pavement to icy salt: a four-season gear basket earns its keep.

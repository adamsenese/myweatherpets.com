Bangkok weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Bangkok's 'cool' season is not cool: December, the mildest month, still averages a 90.1F high, sitting right at the 90F dangerous line for dog walks.
  • April is the brutal peak with average highs of 96.3F, and the city's record of 105.8F was set on May 7, 2023.
  • Nights offer little relief: average lows range from 73.8F to 81.0F year round, so pavement stays warm even before sunrise.
  • Monsoon rains from roughly May through October shave a few degrees off the highs (91.9F in August versus 96.3F in April) but repay it with humidity that keeps heat stress high for dogs.

Hartford weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Hartford averages 51.7 inches of snow a year, and February alone brings 14.8 inches, so paw protection and salt rinses are winter routine for Connecticut dogs.
  • January lows in Hartford average 18.8F, below the 20F line where walks turn dangerous, so midwinter dogs should go out coated, booted, and brief.
  • Summer is no afterthought: July highs average 85.2F, above the 82F risky band, and Connecticut River Valley humidity pushes the feels-like temperature higher still.
  • Hartford's records run from 103F down to -26F, one of New England's wider spreads, so local pets see nearly every kind of weather the Northeast can produce.

Minneapolis weather facts every pet owner should know

  • January in Minneapolis averages a high of just 23.6F and a low of 8.8F, both below the 20F dangerous line for many dogs, so midwinter walks are short, geared-up affairs.
  • The city averages 51.2 inches of snow a year, with December alone bringing 11.4 inches, prime season for snow-loving huskies and paw-wax appointments for everyone else.
  • Minneapolis has plunged to -41F (January 1888), and frostbite can hit ears, tails, and paws in minutes at such extremes, so know your dog's cold limits.
  • Summers flip the script: July highs average 83.4F and the record is 108F (July 1936), so Minnesota dogs need heat awareness too, especially thick-coated northern breeds.

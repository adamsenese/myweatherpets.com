Memphis weather facts every pet owner should know

  • July highs in Memphis average 91.9F, and Mississippi River humidity makes it feel hotter, so summer dog walks belong at sunrise when lows average 73.6F.
  • Memphis hit 108F on July 13, 1980; on extreme days like that, even a shaded evening walk can be too much for flat-faced breeds, so plan indoor play instead.
  • Winters are mild by northern standards: January highs average 50.9F and snow totals just 2.7 inches a year, so most dogs walk comfortably all winter with at most a light coat.
  • The muggy warm season is long here, with average highs above 82F from June through September, four full months when the 82F risky-walk threshold is the daily default.

Memphis pet weather questions

June through September, when average highs run 86.0F to 91.9F, midday walks sit between risky and dangerous, and Memphis humidity makes those numbers feel worse. Walk before 8 am, when temperatures are closer to the 70F to 73.6F overnight averages, and save fetch for air conditioning.
Dogs cool by panting, and humid air makes panting less effective, so a humid 85F day in Memphis can stress a dog as much as a dry 95F day. With July lows averaging 73.6F, even early mornings are muggy in midsummer: bring water, watch for heavy panting, and shorten routes for seniors and flat-faced breeds.

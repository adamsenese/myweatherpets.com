Brisbane weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Brisbane's subtropical summer is serious for dogs: January highs average 86.7F, past the 82F risky line, and coastal humidity means dawn walks are the default from November to March.
  • Winter is the payoff: July highs average 71.6F with lows of 50.9F, so even midday winter walks sit comfortably below the 77F caution threshold.
  • The record high of 107F was set on February 22, 2004, and hot pavement lingers into the evening here, so the back-of-hand pavement test matters even after sunset in a heat wave.
  • Brisbane has never had city snow, and its record low at the current station is a gentle 37F, so most local dogs will never need more than a token winter sweater.

Brisbane pet weather questions

Most of summer: January highs average 86.7F, above the 82F risky line, and humid nights that only fall to 71.1F limit evening relief. From November through March, walk at dawn, keep sessions short, and treat 90F-plus days, up to the record 107F, as indoor days.
Rarely. July lows average 50.9F, above the 45F coat threshold, and winter highs of 71.6F are ideal walking weather. Only on unusual cold snaps near the station record of 37F would a small or senior dog want a light layer for early morning outings.
Summer combines 86.7F average January heat with frequent afternoon thunderstorms, so the morning walk is doubly important. Keep anxious dogs indoors when storms build, and after rain remember humid 80F-plus air still stresses dogs even though the pavement has cooled.

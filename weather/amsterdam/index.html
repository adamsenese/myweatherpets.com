Amsterdam weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Amsterdam's average July high is just 72.5F, below the 77F caution line, so canal-side walks are dog-safe virtually all summer (the record is 97.3F).
  • Rain is the real weather story: about 133.2 days a year get at least 1 mm, peaking in August at 98.6 mm, so a drying towel is the most-used pet accessory in town.
  • The city averages about 7.0 inches of snow a year, with February the snowiest month at roughly 2.1 inches, enough for a few magical canal-snow walks and salty-paw rinses.
  • January lows average 34.2F with humidity near 87 percent, damp cold that justifies coats for small, senior, and short-coated dogs below 45F.

Amsterdam pet weather questions

January lows average 34.2F, so most dogs are fine with a coat for small or senior pups below 45F. Take real care in rare cold waves, since Amsterdam has dropped to -3.5F, and the 87 percent winter humidity makes freezing days feel colder.
Rarely: July's average high is 72.5F, comfortably under the 77F caution line. During occasional heatwaves the city has reached 97.3F, and on those days switch to early-morning canal loops and shaded parks.
With about 133.2 rainy days a year and August alone averaging 98.6 mm, wet walks are unavoidable. A dog raincoat for short-haired breeds, towel-downs at the door, and drying ears well after soaked walks keep skin and coat healthy.

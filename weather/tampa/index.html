Tampa weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Tampa's average highs top 90F for four straight months, from June (90.5F) through September (90.2F), so summer dog walks live at dawn here.
  • Thanks to the bay's moderating water, Tampa never officially hit 100F until July 27, 2025, the first time in its recorded history.
  • Even Tampa's summer nights stay tropical: July lows average 76.6F, so flat-faced breeds and seniors need short, shady outings even before sunrise.
  • Cold gear mostly stays in the closet: January lows average 52.8F, snow does not accumulate in an average year, and the all-time low is 18F (December 13, 1962).

São Paulo weather facts every pet owner should know

  • São Paulo's summer peaks in February with average highs of 84.2F, past the 82F risky line for dog walks, and humid subtropical air makes it feel hotter, so dawn is the safe summer slot.
  • Winter is walking season: July highs average 73.2F with lows of 55F, comfortable enough that most dogs can enjoy long midday outings from June through August.
  • Perched about 785 m above sea level, the city has never seen lasting snow, and its record low at the Mirante de Santana station is a modest 28F, so heavy winter coats are unnecessary for most breeds.
  • Famous for its fine drizzle, the 'terra da garoa' gets frequent light rain, so a quick towel-dry after walks helps prevent damp-coat chills in the 55F winter nights.

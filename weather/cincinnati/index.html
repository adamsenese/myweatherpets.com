Cincinnati weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Cincinnati winters dip to average January lows of 23.1F, in the caution band for dogs, and the all-time record is a brutal -25F from January 1977.
  • Summer afternoons average 86.0F in July with Ohio Valley humidity, so midday walks give way to morning and evening loops.
  • Cincinnati averages 23.3 inches of snow per year, with January the snowiest at 7.7 inches: enough to bring out sidewalk salt, so rinse paws after winter walks.
  • Built on hills above the Ohio River, Cincinnati's valley traps summer humidity, which makes an 86F afternoon feel hotter to a panting dog.

Cincinnati pet weather questions

Seoul weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Seoul swings roughly 50 degrees between seasons: January highs average just 35.8F while August reaches 86.0F, so dogs here need both a winter coat plan and a summer heat plan.
  • January lows average 22.1F, inside the 20 to 32F caution band, and the record low of -9.6F (observations since 1907) means wind chill can push walks into dangerous territory fast.
  • Seoul averages about 24 snow days a year, roughly 7 of them in January alone, so road salt rinses and paw checks are standard winter routine.
  • The record high of 103.3F came in August, and the East Asian monsoon in June and July adds humidity that makes an 84.2F July afternoon feel far hotter to a dog.

Baton Rouge weather facts every pet owner should know

  • August in Baton Rouge averages a 92.2F high and a 73.3F low, so pavement stays paw-scorching into the evening: dawn is the only reliably safe summer walk window.
  • Average highs exceed 90F from June (90.5F) through July (91.9F) and August (92.2F), three-plus months where midday dog walks sit in the danger zone.
  • Snow averages just 0.2 inches a year, so Baton Rouge dogs trade snow booties for cooling mats and shaded porches.
  • Winter is the reward season: January highs average 62.3F, perfect for long walks along the Mississippi River levee downtown.

Baton Rouge pet weather questions

June through August average highs of 90.5F to 92.2F, all above the 90F danger threshold, and Louisiana humidity makes it feel even hotter to dogs. Walk at sunrise near the 73.3F August average low, keep midday trips to shaded grass, and palm-test pavement first.
With summer lows holding near 71.5F to 73.8F, the air stays muggy around the clock and panting cools dogs poorly. Treat humid days above 77F as caution territory and give flat-faced breeds indoor play instead of afternoon walks.

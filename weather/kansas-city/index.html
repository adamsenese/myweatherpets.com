Kansas City weather facts every pet owner should know

  • Kansas City swings nearly 50 degrees between seasons: July highs average 88.3F while January highs average just 38.4F, so pet owners need both a heat plan and a cold plan.
  • The city's records run from 113F (August 14, 1936) down to -23F (December 22 and 23, 1989), one of the widest official spreads of any major US city.
  • About 18.2 inches of snow falls in an average year, peaking at 5.9 inches in February, so keep paw balm or booties handy for salted sidewalks.
  • July nights only cool to an average 68.1F, and Midwest humidity makes that feel warmer, so even morning summer walks call for water breaks.

Kansas City pet weather questions

June through August, when highs average 84.2F to 88.3F, midday walks regularly cross the 82F risky line, and humid days push the feels-like temperature past 90F. Shift walks to early morning, when July lows average 68.1F.
January lows average 19.5F, which is below the 20-32F caution band and into dangerous territory for small or short-coated dogs. Keep outings brief, add a coat below 45F for vulnerable dogs, and watch for ice-melt salt on paws.
An average winter brings 18.2 inches, with February the snowiest month at 5.9 inches. Wipe paws after walks to remove de-icing chemicals, and trim fur between paw pads to prevent ice balls.
